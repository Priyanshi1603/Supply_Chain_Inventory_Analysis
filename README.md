# 📦 Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations and inventory performance using a dataset containing 15,000 records. The objective is to evaluate inventory levels, supplier performance, delivery efficiency, warehouse operations, and product profitability to generate actionable business insights.

The project was completed using Excel, SQL Server, and Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze inventory levels and stock availability
* Identify understock and overstock situations
* Evaluate supplier performance based on shipping time
* Analyze warehouse-wise revenue contribution
* Identify top-selling and most profitable products
* Monitor delivery performance and shipping efficiency
* Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

##  Key Insights

### Business Performance

* Generated ₹3.08 Billion in revenue and ₹612.92 Million in profit.
* Total units sold exceeded 2.22 Million units.

### Inventory Analysis

* Approximately 80% of products were adequately stocked.
* Around 20% of products were identified as understocked.

### Product Performance

* Camera emerged as the most profitable product.
* Laptop and Fan were among the top-performing products.

### Supplier Performance

* Supplier C recorded the lowest average shipping time.
* Supplier E showed the highest average shipping time.

### Warehouse Performance

* Bangalore generated the highest revenue among all warehouse locations.
* Revenue contribution remained balanced across warehouses.

### Delivery Analysis

* Average shipping time was approximately 5.5 days.
* Order distribution remained relatively consistent across shipping durations.

---

##  Recommendations

* Improve inventory planning for understocked products.
* Monitor supplier delivery performance regularly.
* Focus on high-performing products and categories to maximize profitability.
* Continuously track warehouse performance to improve operational efficiency.
